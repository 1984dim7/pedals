Specs

Water Wobble splits your signal in two. One path stays dry. The other runs through a bucket-brigade delay chip whose delay time is swept up and down, which bends the pitch. DIVE sets the balance: mixed, the two make a chorus; the wobbling path alone is a vibrato.

Circuit
Analog. A bucket-brigade delay (BBD) chip swept by a triangle-wave LFO.
FLOW
Speed of the wobble, from a slow drift to a quick shimmer.
DEPTH
How far the pitch swings.
DIVE
Dry / wet mix. Low: a chorus under your dry tone. All the way up: wet only, a vibrato.
CLEAR
Treble of the wobbling path only. Your dry tone is left as it is.
Footswitch
On / off. Four LEDs inside light up while the effect is on.
Power
9V DC, center negative, from a regulated pedal supply (not included).
Jacks
Mono in and out on the sides, power at the top.
Size
About 120 × 65 mm (without knobs and jacks).
Build
Assembled by hand in Japan. Every unit is function-tested before it ships.
